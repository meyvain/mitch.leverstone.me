Meet Mitch — research lead, ethnographer, innovation & strategist.

I help shape product strategy and align cross-functional teams around evidence-based decisions. My expertise lies in healthcare / MedTech organisations where I work to de-risk early-stage innovation by putting user experience at the centre of product strategy, translating ambiguous challenges into clear innovation roadmaps.

My expertise spans Quant & Qual, as I work across the full research spectrum, end-to-end — from deep ethnographic observation to global-scale surveys and ideation and prioritisation workshops with key business stakeholders — translating complex human realities into strategic direction that product / business stakeholders can act on.

The result is faster alignment, better-framed problems, and innovation built on evidence rather than assumptions.

I'm an audiobook listener, escape room enthusiast (I've even designed a few, which were smashing if I might add), and a mother of identical twins who are the least alike you'll ever meet.
